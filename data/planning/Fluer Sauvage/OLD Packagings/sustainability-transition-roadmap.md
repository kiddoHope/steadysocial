## Phase 2: Hybrid Transition (Months 4-6)

### Objective:
Gradually introduce new sustainable bottles while phasing out old inventory.

### Inventory Split Strategy:
```
Month 4:   60% Heritage (old) / 40% Modern (new)
Month 5:   50% Heritage (old) / 50% Modern (new)
Month 6:   25% Heritage clearance / 75% Modern standard
```

### New Bottles Specifications:
- **Material:** Recycled PET or frosted glass (minimum 30% post-consumer recycled content)
- **Supplier partners:** 
  - Primary: Local Philippine manufacturers with sustainability certifications
  - Secondary: International suppliers for overflow orders
- **Lead time:** 4-6 weeks production + shipping
- **Minimum order quantity:** 200 units per color/design

### Pricing Adjustments:
| SKU Type | Regular Price | Positioning |
|----------|--------------|-------------|
| Modern Collection (30ml) | ₱380 | Standard |
| Modern Collection (50ml) | ₱410-420 | Standard |
| Limited Editions | +₱50-100 | Special occasions |

### Discontinuation of Heritage Line:
**Trigger:** When old bottle inventory reaches <30 units remaining  
**Transition Timeline:**
- Week 1: Announce "Heritage Collection final drop" 
- Week 2: Begin promotional pricing (-15% off)
- Week 4: Complete sell-out with final numbered editions
- End of Month 6: Heritage line formally retired from website

### Sustainability Story Evolution:
```
Phase 1 (Months 1-3): "Zero waste - reusing what we started with"
Phase 2 (Months 4-6): "Growing sustainably - balancing heritage and innovation"
Phase 3 (Months 7+): "Full sustainability leadership - certified eco-friendly production"
```

## Phase 3: Complete Transition (Months 7-9)

### Objective:
Fully transition to new sustainable bottle inventory while maintaining premium positioning.

### New Production Standards:
```
Material Requirements:
- Glass bottles: Minimum 30% post-consumer recycled content
- PET bottles: 50%+ rPET (recycled polyethylene terephthalate)
- Labels: FSC-certified paper or recyclable synthetic with soy-based inks
- Caps: Rattan-alternatives or recycled plastic
```

### Sustainability Certifications to Pursue:

#### Option A: CarbonNeutral® Program
- **Certification:** Verified carbon offset for manufacturing and shipping
- **Timeline:** 2-3 months application and verification
- **Cost:** ~$400-600 per ton of CO2 offset + $250 annual fee
- **Benefits:**
  - "Carbon Neutral" badge for website
  - Annual sustainability report to customers
  - Third-party verified emissions reduction

#### Option B: FSC Certification (for wood/plastic packaging)
- **Certification:** Forest Stewardship Council compliance
- **Timeline:** 3-6 months certification process
- **Cost:** $1,500-2,500 annual certification fee
- **Benefits:**
  - Ensures sustainable sourcing of materials
  - Chain-of-custody documentation
  - Resale eligibility for eco-conscious retailers

#### Option C: B-Corp Certification (overall brand)
- **Certification:** Comprehensive social and environmental performance
- **Timeline:** 6-12 months application process
- **Cost:** $5,000-7,000 certification fee
- **Benefits:**
  - Endorsed by global B-Corp community
  - Stronger brand differentiation
  - Attracts impact-focused customers and investors

### Recommended Priority Path:
```
Month 7-8: Pursue CarbonNeutral® (lower cost, faster results)
Month 9-10: Apply for FSC certification on packaging materials
Month 12+: Begin B-Corp eligibility evaluation (long-term goal)
```

### Packaging Innovation:
```
Standard Collection Packaging:
- Modular 3D-printed display stand (reuseable for years)
- Seed paper sleeve (plantable after use)
- Minimal plastic usage in shipping materials
- Recyclable box design with QR code for brand story

Advanced Sustainability Options:
- Compostable mailer bags (PLA-based)
- Water-soluble labels for easy recycling
- Bamboo fiber inserts as alternatives to synthetic
```

## Risk Assessment & Mitigation

| Phase | Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation Strategy |
|-------|------|-------------|--------|---------------------|
| Phase 1 | Customer backlash at Heritage pricing | Medium | High | Emphasize sustainability story; offer trade-in program after purchase |
| Phase 1 | Slower sell-through of old bottles | Medium | Medium | Create bundle deals with regular collection; limited-time discount max 10% after 45 days |
| Phase 2 | Supply chain delays for new bottles | Low-Medium | High | Partner with multiple suppliers; maintain safety stock buffer |
| Phase 2 | Quality inconsistency between old/new bottles | Medium | Medium | Enhanced QC standards for both inventory types |
| Phase 3 | Certification costs exceed budget | Low | Medium | Start with CarbonNeutral® (lower cost) before pursuing FSC or B-Corp |
| All Phases | Environmental greenwashing accusations | Low-Medium | Critical | Full transparency; publish sourcing details; third-party verification |
