The Core Concept

An AI Agent isn't just a passive generator or static chatbot responding to inputs. It is an autonomous entity capable of perceiving its environment, making decisions, and executing multi-step actions to achieve distinct goals.

"If standard GenAI is an engine, an AI Agent is the entire vehicle, equipped with a GPS, map, and automated steering wheel."
📌 THE VISCERAL LOOP

The Agentic Cycle

1
Perceive: Gathers sensors/data environment logs
2
Reason: Brain breaks goals down into paths
3
Act: Executes via external tools/API scripts

The Sketchy Timeline of Origins ⏳

1950s: Cybernetics Foundations

Alan Turing posits the Imitation Game. Early computer pioneers build structural feedback loops inspired by biological nervous systems.

Keyword: Feedback Loops
1990s: Rational Agents Definition

Russell & Norvig publish AI standard text explicitly framing artificial intelligence as the study of **Rational Agents** reacting optimally to environments.

Keyword: Rule-Based Logic
2020s: LLM-Driven ReAct 🚀

Large Language Models emerge as the reasoning core. Papers like **ReAct** (Reason+Act) unleash agents using web browsing, terminal compilation, and custom tooling.

Keyword: Tool Use & Planning
